## 📦 What's Inside

This research project addresses the key requirements for understanding and building a rental management system that serves both landlords and tenants transparently and effectively in Kenya.

- ✅ **10 Real Tenancy Agreements** analyzed from Kenya (residential, commercial, student housing, co-living)
- ✅ **9 Linked CSV Tables** with 6 months of realistic operational data
- ✅ **Complete Workflow Documentation** covering 20+ steps from enquiry to deposit refund
- ✅ **Legal Compliance Coverage** - Rent Restriction Act (Cap 296), Landlord & Tenant Act (Cap 301), Data Protection Act 2019
- ✅ **Kenya-Specific Details** - M-Pesa payments, KRA tax compliance, Nairobi/Mombasa market data

## 📋 Deliverables

### 1. Tenancy Agreements Research (`/research/tenancy-agreements-analysis.md`)
- Collection and analysis of 10 real Kenyan tenancy agreements
- Coverage of residential (low-cost, mid-range, high-end) and commercial properties

### 2. Excel Dataset (`/data/rental-management-dataset.xlsx`)

  - Units
  - Tenants
  - Staff
  - Tenancies
  - Charges
  - Payments
  - Meter Readings
  - Events
- Ready to be used as seed data for system development

### 3. Lifecycle Documentation (`/research/tenant-management-lifecycle.md`)
- Complete tenant lifecycle from enquiry to move-out
- Management workflows and processes
- Identification of automation opportunities
- Document and record requirements at each stage

## Legal Framework

The system design considers:
- **Rent Restriction Act (Cap 296)** - Controlled residential tenancies
- **Landlord and Tenant (Shops, Hotels and Catering Establishments) Act (Cap 301)** - Commercial tenancies
- **Distress for Rent Act (Cap 293)** - Rent recovery procedures
- **Land Act 2012** - Property registration and ownership
- **Data Protection Act 2019** - Tenant personal data protection

## Key Principles

1. **Transparency**: The system should inform and educate tenants about their rights and obligations
2. **Quality Data**: Accurate, consistent record-keeping prevents disputes
3. **Legal Compliance**: All processes must align with Kenyan law
4. **User-Friendly**: Designed for Kenya's young, growing population learning about tenancies
